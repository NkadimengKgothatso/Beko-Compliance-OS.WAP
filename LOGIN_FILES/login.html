About the Platform

Beko ComplianceOS is a South African legal-tech platform built to help businesses navigate their regulatory responsibilities with SARS, CIPC, and other relevant authorities. Our mission is to cut through the complexity of compliance by clearly showing users what's required, when it's due, and how to get it done right.

What We Offer

  • Monitoring of compliance obligations and key deadlines
  • Automated reminders via SMS, email, and in-app notifications
  • Clear, step-by-step guidance for completing compliance tasks
  • Access to legal templates and essential business documents
  • Plain-language legal and tax education for business owners
